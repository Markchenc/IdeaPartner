# Stage 1: positioning and review plan

Identify the researcher's actual claims and scope. Classify contribution and maturity only to select useful review questions. Do not research yet.

Produce idea_claims with original-input line ranges, boundaries, maturity, contribution_type, constraints, working_assumptions and prioritized review_questions. Each question states how the answer could change a judgment. Prefer a short plan (roughly 500–800 Chinese characters, or equivalent concise prose); do not omit essential distinctions to meet a count.

Missing constraints remain unknown. Use explicit assumptions or conditional interpretations for consequential ambiguity. Do not invent contributions or demand a complete method for an early idea. Continue automatically; do not solicit positioning confirmation.

The output is a compact task brief, not an analysis report or a precompiled exhaustive search matrix. See the plan section of artifact-contracts.md.
